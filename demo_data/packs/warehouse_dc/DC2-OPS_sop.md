# DC-2 operations SOP (fictional, for SHIFTTRACE demos)

## 1.1 Zones and locations
Zone B is the ambient pick zone for grocery cases. Pick-face locations are written B-aisle-level, for example B-04-2 (aisle 4, level 2). Reserve pallets are held in the high bay, written R-number, for example R-12. The cold room (CR-1) holds chilled stock at 2 to 8 degrees C. Every stock movement between locations is a stock transfer and must be posted to the ERP.

## 2.1 Pick-face replenishment
Each pick face has a minimum quantity printed on its label. When a pick face falls below its minimum, replenish it from reserve with a stock transfer before the next pick wave. Move whole cases only. For SKU-2231 (basmati rice 5 kg, case of 4) the pick-face minimum at B-04-2 is 10 cases and the maximum is 30 cases.

## 2.2 Stock transfers and ERP posting
A stock transfer names the SKU, the quantity, the from location and the to location. The associate confirms the transfer after it is read back; only then is stock moved in the system and the transfer posted to the ERP, which returns an ERP reference. Never move stock in the system that was not physically moved. A transfer that would leave a negative quantity is refused.

## 3.1 Cycle counts and discrepancies
If a physical count differs from the system quantity, record the counted quantity and raise a discrepancy. Do not adjust the system quantity during the shift. Recount on the next shift with a second associate; if the difference remains, the inventory controller investigates mis-picks and unrecorded transfers.

## 3.2 Cold room temperature limits
The cold room must stay between 2 and 8 degrees C. Above 8 degrees C for more than 15 minutes, move chilled stock to the backup cold room and call facilities. Record the temperature at the start of every shift.

## 4.1 Inbound delays and receiving
A delayed inbound shipment must be chased with the carrier using the shipment ID and the carrier reference. If the ETA moves past the next pick wave, tell the shift lead so replenishment can be planned from reserve. Receive against the advance shipping notice and record shortages and damage before signing.

## 5.1 Forklift and pedestrian safety
Pedestrians stop at aisle ends and make eye contact with the forklift driver before crossing. Never stand or walk under a raised load. Forklifts give way to pedestrians in marked walkways. Report any near miss to the shift lead the same shift.

## 5.2 Working at height
Never climb racking. Above level 2, use the order picker with a harness clipped to the anchor point. Check the harness and lanyard before each use.
