# Northbreeze NB-AH20 Air Handling Unit: Service Manual (excerpt)

## 1.1 Overview
The NB-AH20 is a draw-through air handling unit with a G4 panel pre-filter, an F7 bag filter, a chilled-water cooling coil with condensate tray, and a belt-driven supply fan (motor 7.5 kW, nameplate 14.2 A). The controller shows the active alarm, supply air temperature and filter differential pressure.

## 2.1 Controller alarms
AL-5 FILTER DIRTY: bag filter differential pressure above 250 Pa. AL-8 FAN FAIL: no airflow proven within 60 seconds of a fan start, often a broken or slipping belt. AL-11 FROST PROTECT: coil leaving air below 4 °C. An alarm lamp does not identify the cause; inspect the item named by the alarm.

## 3.1 Weekly inspection round readings and limits
Record each reading with its value: (1) G4 pre-filter differential pressure, clean below 100 Pa, change at 150 Pa; (2) F7 bag filter differential pressure, clean below 150 Pa, change at 250 Pa (AL-5); (3) supply air temperature within 1.5 °C of setpoint; (4) supply fan motor current, normal 10 to 13 A, investigate above 13.5 A; (5) condensate tray, no standing water and drain trap flowing; (6) fan belt, no squeal at start-up and no visible glazing or cracking; (7) unusual noise or vibration at the fan bearings. Any reading outside its limit must be logged as an open item for the maintenance planner.

## 3.2 Filter replacement
Replace the G4 pre-filter panels (part FLT-G4-PANEL) and the F7 bag filter set (part FLT-F7-592) with the fan isolated. Fit bags with pockets vertical. Record the differential pressure after restart; a new F7 set reads 60 to 90 Pa.

## 4.1 Fan belt tension and alignment
The supply fan uses two SPA-1250 V-belts (part BLT-SPA-1250), always replaced as a matched pair. Correct tension: 16 mm deflection at mid-span under 25 N. Squeal at start-up usually means low tension or glazed belts. Check pulley alignment with a straight edge after any belt change.

## 4.2 Fan bearing lubrication
Regrease fan bearings every 4,000 hours with 10 g of EP2 grease (part GRS-EP2) while rotating slowly by hand with the fan isolated.

## 5.1 Isolation and rotating parts
Before opening any access door: stop the unit from the controller, switch off and lock the local isolator, and wait until the fan has fully stopped. The fan can windmill from duct airflow; chock the fan wheel before touching belts. Never open the fan section door while the unit is running.
