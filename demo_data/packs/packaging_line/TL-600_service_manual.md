# Trackline TL-600 Belt Conveyor: Service Manual (excerpt)

## 1.1 Overview and ratings
The TL-600 is a 600 mm wide flat-belt conveyor with a 5.5 kW geared motor at the head end, a crowned tail pulley with screw take-ups for tracking and tension, and a photo-eye PE-4 at the outfeed for jam detection. Nameplate motor current is 11.0 A at full load. Rated belt speed 10 to 36 m/min.

## 2.1 Controller fault codes
F-12 DRIVE OVERLOAD: the motor protection relay tripped on sustained current above 110 percent of nameplate (12.1 A). Common causes, in order: (1) belt rubbing the frame because of tracking drift; (2) product debris packed under the belt at the tail pulley; (3) gearbox oil low or wrong grade, raising drag; (4) a seized idler or tail pulley bearing; (5) belt over-tensioned after a tracking adjustment. F-07 PHOTO-EYE BLOCKED: PE-4 dirty or misaligned, or a real jam. F-03 E-STOP ACTIVE. Reset only after the cause is found; repeated resets can burn the motor.

## 3.1 Belt tracking adjustment
Belt drift is corrected at the tail pulley take-up screws. Adjust in quarter turns only, at most one full turn in total per adjustment, on the side the belt is running towards. Run the empty belt for 5 minutes and re-check before adjusting again. If more than one full turn has already been applied and the belt still drifts, stop adjusting: inspect the belt splice for a crooked or damaged lacing, and check for debris on the tail pulley face. Over-adjustment over-tensions one edge and raises motor current.

## 3.2 Belt tension
Correct tension allows 10 to 15 mm deflection at mid-span under hand pressure. Measure both edges; a difference above 3 mm indicates uneven take-up adjustment. Over-tension raises motor current and bearing load.

## 4.1 Monthly preventive maintenance checklist
Work through these steps in order and record each one: (1) lock out the drive at isolator MCC-2 and verify zero energy (section 6.1); (2) inspect the belt surface and splice lacing for cracks, lifted clips or fraying; (3) clean debris from the tail pulley and under the return belt; (4) check the gearbox oil level at the sight glass, top up with GX-220 gear oil if below the mark (section 4.2); (5) check belt tension at mid-span (section 3.2); (6) clean the photo-eye PE-4 lens and confirm the alignment LED is steady; (7) refit all guards, remove locks and run the empty belt for 5 minutes; (8) record motor current at full load and confirm belt tracking is centred within 10 mm.

## 4.2 Gearbox oil
The head-end gearbox takes 1.2 litres of GX-220 synthetic gear oil (part OIL-GX220). Level must be at the middle of the sight glass with the conveyor stopped. Low oil raises drag and motor current and can trip F-12. Change the oil every 10,000 hours.

## 5.1 Motor current limits
Normal full-load current is 9.0 to 11.0 A. Between 11.0 and 12.1 A sustained, investigate within the shift (tracking, debris, tension, oil). Above 12.1 A the relay trips with F-12. Record current at full load after any tracking or tension adjustment.

## 6.1 Lockout and guarding
Before touching the belt, pulleys, take-ups or gearbox: stop the line, isolate the drive at MCC-2, apply a personal lock and tag, and verify zero energy by pressing START. Never adjust tracking with guards removed while the belt is running. Refit every guard before restoring power.
